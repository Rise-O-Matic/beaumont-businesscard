Beaumont Library District Business Card Color Builder

Customize your BLD business card colors · 3.5″ × 2″ standard

How to use: Choose colors for each element using the swatches below each card. Click directly on the Name, Job Title, Phone, and Email fields on the back face to type your own text. Use your own direct line, the main library line, or a number with an extension — whatever fits. Uncheck Show main number under address to avoid repeating the main number in both contact areas. Use Randomize to generate a random color combination, or Reset to return to the default theme. When you’re happy with a design, click Add to Gallery to save it. Saved cards appear in the gallery below where you can compare, rename, or delete them. Use Export PDF to download a print-ready file with crop marks and bleed.
